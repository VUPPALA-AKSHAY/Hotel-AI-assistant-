## FAQ

### What time is check-in?
Answer: Check-in is from 3:00 PM. If you arrive earlier, we will try to get you a room as soon as it is ready; before 12 PM it is free subject to availability.

### What time is check-out?
Answer: Check-out is at 11:00 AM. Late check-out is subject to availability (50% of one night until 6 PM, a full night after 6 PM).

### Is breakfast included in the room rate?
Answer: Yes. Breakfast is included in all room bookings as a buffet at Coastal Spice (7:00-10:30 AM) or via in-room continental breakfast until 11:00 AM.

### Does the hotel have a swimming pool?
Answer: Yes, we have a lagoon-style swimming pool with a separate kids' area, open from 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM. It is complimentary for all guests.

### Which room is suitable for three guests?
Answer: The Seaview Balcony Room fits up to 3 guests (a rollaway can be added). For four guests, the Family Suite with a sofa bed is ideal.

### What is the cancellation policy?
Answer: Bookings can be cancelled free of charge up to 72 hours before check-in. Cancellations inside 72 hours or no-shows are charged one night. Peak-period stays are non-refundable after 14 days before check-in.

### Do you provide airport pickup?
Answer: Yes. Private airport transfers from Dabolim Airport cost 1500 INR per car and can be booked via the front desk or WhatsApp at least 24 hours ahead.

### Is Wi-Fi free?
Answer: Yes, high-speed Wi-Fi (100 Mbps) is complimentary throughout the property, including in all rooms.

### Is parking available?
Answer: Yes, complimentary valet parking is available for all guests, with capacity for 70 vehicles.

### Does the hotel allow pets?
Answer: Pets are not allowed inside the hotel. Service animals are permitted with prior notice at the front desk.

### What languages do the staff speak?
Answer: Our staff speaks English, Kannada, and Hindi. Front-desk staff can also arrange basic assistance in other languages on request.

### Are children allowed, and is there a charge?
Answer: Children under 6 stay and eat free from the buffet. Children aged 6-12 are charged 50% on meals. An extra bed costs 1500 INR per night in select room types.

### Is there a restaurant in the hotel?
Answer: Yes. We have Coastal Spice (Goan and Indian, all meals), Azure Terrace (Pan-Asian and Continental, dinner), the Palm Bar (evening drinks and tapas), and a Poolside Cafe (all day).

### Does the hotel have a spa?
Answer: Yes, the Wellness Spa offers Ayurvedic, deep-tissue, and aromatherapy massages from 2500 INR per session. Bookings are recommended 24 hours in advance.

### Can I hold a business meeting here?
Answer: Yes. The Business Centre has a meeting room for up to 10 people at 1200 INR per hour, with printing and videoconferencing available.

### Do you have beach access?
Answer: Yes, the hotel has a private stretch of Candolim beach via the Beach Club, open 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM with complimentary loungers and umbrellas.

### What are the quiet hours?
Answer: Quiet hours run from 10:00 PM to 6:00 AM across the property, so all guests can rest.

### Is the hotel disabled accessible?
Answer: Yes. All public areas, the Deluxe rooms, restaurants, and the Beach Club are wheelchair accessible. Accessible rooms are available on request.

### Can I pay by UPI or card?
Answer: Yes. We accept Visa, Mastercard, RuPay, Amex, UPI, and cash.

### Do you offer laundry service?
Answer: Yes, same-day laundry from 250 INR and dry cleaning from 450 INR, with collection before 10:00 AM and delivery by 9:00 PM.
